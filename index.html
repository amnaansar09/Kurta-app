Five design principles

    Everyday difficulties, designed out

    DifficultyHow Aasani helps
    Raising armsFront-opening construction
    Shoulder stiffnessWide armholes and relaxed sleeves
    Painful finger jointsConcealed magnets or large snaps
    Reaching behindNo fastenings at the back, ever
    BendingEvery fastening is at the front, within easy reach
    Changing body shapeAdjustable side panels
    Depending on othersDesigned so you can dress yourself wherever possible

    Who it's for

    Women aged 55 and above, women living with arthritis or joint stiffness, women recovering from surgery, and the daughters, sons and caregivers who shop for them.

    A note on magnets

    Aasani magnets are sealed inside fabric pockets and never touch the skin. If you have a pacemaker, ICD or other implanted device, choose the large snaps, soft hook-and-loop or ring-pull zip instead, and check with your doctor.
